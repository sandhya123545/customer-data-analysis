# Retail Customer Insights | Customer Data Analysis

## 📌 Project Overview
This project analyzes customer shopping data to understand purchasing behaviour, product preferences, payment methods, and revenue patterns across different shopping malls in Istanbul.

The analysis was performed using **MySQL Workbench** for data analysis and **Power BI** for interactive dashboard creation.

## 🎯 Project Objectives
- Analyze customer shopping behaviour by gender and age group.
- Identify popular product categories.
- Compare revenue and products sold across customer segments.
- Understand customer payment method preferences.
- Compare revenue across shopping malls.
- Visualize key business metrics through an interactive dashboard.

## 🛠️ Tools & Technologies
- **MySQL Workbench** – Data querying and analysis
- **Power BI** – Data visualization and dashboard development
- **SQL** – Aggregations, filtering, grouping, and analytical queries

## 📊 Dataset Information
- **Dataset:** Istanbul Shopping Malls Customer Transactions
- **Period:** 2021–2023
- **Records:** 99,458
- **Columns:** 10
- **Missing values:** None identified during initial inspection
- **Duplicate records:** None identified during initial inspection

The dataset includes customer details, product categories, quantities, prices, payment methods, invoice dates, and shopping mall names.

## 📈 Key Analysis Areas
- Customer transactions by gender
- Products sold and revenue by gender
- Products sold by category
- Transactions and purchasing patterns by age group
- Revenue by age group
- Transactions by payment method
- Revenue by shopping mall
- Revenue trends over time

## 📊 Power BI Dashboard
The Power BI report includes KPI cards, charts, and slicers to help users explore customer shopping patterns and compare results across different segments.

**Dashboard KPIs:**
- Total Revenue
- Total Products Sold
- Sum of Price
- Total Transactions
